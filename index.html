Botox results typically last between 3 to 4 months, depending on your individual metabolism, muscle activity, and the treatment area. Many clients schedule follow-up appointments to maintain their results. During your consultation, we'll discuss a treatment schedule that works best for your goals.
Most clients experience little to no downtime after dermal filler treatments. You may notice mild swelling, redness, or bruising at the injection site, which usually resolves within a few days. We'll provide you with clear aftercare instructions to help you heal comfortably and quickly.
Kybella is an FDA-approved injectable that contains deoxycholic acid, a naturally occurring molecule that helps break down and absorb fat. During a mesotherapy session, a series of fine micro-injections deliver a customized blend of vitamins, minerals, and active ingredients directly into the skin or targeted fat areas. Sessions are quick, generally well-tolerated, and require minimal recovery time. Results build gradually with a recommended series of treatments.
